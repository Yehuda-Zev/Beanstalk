Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This site makes a personalized storybook PDF from a photo you upload. It is a student project.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your Google account ID, email address, and name. We use these only to log you in and to show you your own books. We do not request any other access to your Google account.
  • What you enter: the child's name and any "About me" text you type.
  • Your photo: it is sent to an AI image service (OpenAI) to draw the book's pictures. We do not keep the photo after the book is made.
  • Your books: each generated PDF is saved so you can return and download it later.
  • Logs: basic server logs (time, page requested, IP address, errors) to keep the site working and prevent abuse.

How we use it

Only to run the site: sign you in, make your book, and show it to you again. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who else sees it

OpenAI receives the photo and story prompts to generate images. Our hosting and database providers store the site's data. No one else has access.

Deleting your data

Email us at the address below and we will delete your account and books.
